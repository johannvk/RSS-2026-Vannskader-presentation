  \vspace{1.0cm}
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.52\textwidth}
      \textit{Conclusions}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item High-emissions, 2071--2100: national median total
          damages increase by $33\%$
        \item Impact geographically uneven
        \item Greatest impact: Finnmark and southern
          mountain regions
        \item Substantial variation in impact from different climate simulations
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}
      \textit{Caveats}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Insurance data cover reported damages; claims below
          the deductible unobserved
        \item Ignore spatially correlated errors,
          clearly underestimating uncertainty
        \item Not representing short-term extreme precipitation events
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}
